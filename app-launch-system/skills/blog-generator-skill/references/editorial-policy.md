# Editorial Policy

## Topic selection

Prioritize topics where the app has a verified, meaningful contribution. Balance:

- Problem education for users discovering a need
- How-to content for users evaluating a solution
- Product workflows for activation and retention
- Release notes for existing users

Do not create competitor comparison, medical, legal, financial, security, or compliance claims without authoritative sources and explicit scope.

## Required frontmatter

Include `contentId`, `locale`, `status`, `title`, `description`, `slug`, `intent`, `audience`, `publishedAt`, `updatedAt`, `canonical`, `evidence`, `primaryKeyword`, and `relatedPages`. Use `draft` and blank dates until publication is explicitly scheduled.

## Quality gates

- The title and opening answer the assigned intent.
- Every product-specific claim maps to evidence.
- Headings are descriptive and form a coherent outline.
- Steps are executable and limitations are explicit.
- Links resolve or are marked as pending in metadata.
- Metadata is unique and localized.
- The article contains no unresolved placeholders.
