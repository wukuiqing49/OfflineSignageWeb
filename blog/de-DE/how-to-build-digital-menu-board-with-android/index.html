Digitales Menüboard mit Android TV & Tablets ohne monatliche Gebühren erstellen

Schritt-für-Schritt-Anleitung zur Umwandlung von Android TVs in professionelle Menüboards für Gastronomie und Cafés.

2026-08-19 · 6 Min. Lesezeit

Contents

  • Overview

Running a professional digital menu board does not require expensive proprietary hardware or recurring cloud subscription fees. With OfflineSignage, any Android TV, tablet, or media box can become a reliable, local-first menu display in minutes.

Why Choose a Local-First Digital Menu Board?

Traditional cloud digital signage platforms charge monthly subscriptions per screen ($10 - $50/month per display) and stop displaying content or display watermark errors if your internet goes down. A local-first architecture solves this by storing all menu media directly on the Android player device, controlled entirely over your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet network.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Install & Launch OfflineSignage: Download and open OfflineSignage on your Android TV or commercial display. The app automatically enters fullscreen signage mode and displays its local pairing code and LAN IP address.
  2. Connect via Browser: On your phone or laptop, open your browser and enter the on-screen address (e.g., http://192.168.1.105:8080) or scan the QR code. No account registration is required.
  3. Upload Your Menu Graphics: Drag and drop your menu images, daily specials, and video clips into the Web Management Console. Set transition durations (e.g., 10 seconds per category slide).
  4. Start Fullscreen Playback: Tap “Play”. Your Android display immediately starts looping the menu in crystal-clear high definition.
  5. Close the Browser: You can safely close your phone or computer browser. The Android display continues running 24/7 independently from local storage.

💡 Pro Tip: For multi-item menus, split your items across 2–3 high-contrast slides. OfflineSignage supports automatic screen wake-lock, so your menu never sleeps during business hours.
