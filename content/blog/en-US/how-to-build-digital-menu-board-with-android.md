# How to Build a Digital Menu Board with Android TVs and Tablets without Monthly Fees


<p class="lead">Running a professional digital menu board does not require expensive proprietary hardware or recurring cloud subscription fees. With OfflineSignage, any Android TV, tablet, or media box can become a reliable, local-first menu display in minutes.</p>

<h2>Why Choose a Local-First Digital Menu Board?</h2>
<p>Traditional cloud digital signage platforms charge monthly subscriptions per screen ($10 - $50/month per display) and stop displaying content or display watermark errors if your internet goes down. A local-first architecture solves this by storing all menu media directly on the Android player device, controlled entirely over your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet network.</p>

<h2>What You Need</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Screen / Hardware:</strong> Any Android TV (Sony, TCL, Xiaomi, Hisense), Android TV Box (Chromecast with Google TV, Fire TV, Mi Box), commercial signage display, or Android tablet.</li>
  <li><strong>App:</strong> OfflineSignage installed on the Android device.</li>
  <li><strong>Control Device:</strong> Your smartphone, iPad, laptop, or POS computer connected to the same Wi-Fi.</li>
  <li><strong>Menu Media:</strong> 4K/1080p images (PNG/JPG), MP4 promotional video loops, or HTML menu layouts.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Step-by-Step Setup Guide</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Install & Launch OfflineSignage:</strong> Download and open OfflineSignage on your Android TV or commercial display. The app automatically enters fullscreen signage mode and displays its local pairing code and LAN IP address.</li>
  <li><strong>Connect via Browser:</strong> On your phone or laptop, open your browser and enter the on-screen address (e.g., <code>http://192.168.1.105:8080</code>) or scan the QR code. No account registration is required.</li>
  <li><strong>Upload Your Menu Graphics:</strong> Drag and drop your menu images, daily specials, and video clips into the Web Management Console. Set transition durations (e.g., 10 seconds per category slide).</li>
  <li><strong>Start Fullscreen Playback:</strong> Tap &ldquo;Play&rdquo;. Your Android display immediately starts looping the menu in crystal-clear high definition.</li>
  <li><strong>Close the Browser:</strong> You can safely close your phone or computer browser. The Android display continues running 24/7 independently from local storage.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Best Practices for Restaurant Menu Boards</h2>
<p>For multi-item menus, split your items across 2&ndash;3 high-contrast slides. Use landscape orientation for wall-mounted TVs and portrait orientation for counter tablet displays. OfflineSignage supports automatic screen wake-lock, so your menu never sleeps during business hours.</p>
